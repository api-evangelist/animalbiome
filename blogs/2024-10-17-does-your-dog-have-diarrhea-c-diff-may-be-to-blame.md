In dogs diarrhea is an extremely common occurrence. But did you know that many diarrhea cases are caused by a bacterial pathogen called Clostridium difficile (which is commonly called C diff for short)? If you take your sick dog to the veterinarian with a report of frequent diarrhea , your vet will likely run a test on their stool that looks for the presence of particular bacterial pathogens, including Clostridium difficile .
